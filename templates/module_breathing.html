🌬️ Pranayama Techniques

1️⃣

Anulom Vilom (Alternate Nostril Breathing)

A balancing breath that harmonizes left and right brain hemispheres

How to Practice:

  1. Sit comfortably with spine straight
  2. Close right nostril with right thumb
  3. Inhale slowly through left nostril (4 counts)
  4. Close left nostril with ring finger, release right
  5. Exhale through right nostril (4 counts)
  6. Inhale through right nostril (4 counts)
  7. Switch and exhale through left nostril (4 counts)
  8. This completes one round - repeat 10 times
⏱ 3-5 minutes 🎯 Balance & Calm 📊 Beginner Friendly
2️⃣

Kapalbhati (Skull Shining Breath)

Energizing breath that cleanses respiratory system and boosts metabolism

How to Practice:

  1. Sit in comfortable position with straight spine
  2. Take a deep breath in
  3. Exhale forcefully through nose by contracting abdomen
  4. Allow passive inhalation (belly naturally expands)
  5. Repeat rapid exhalations 20-30 times per round
  6. Rest for 30 seconds between rounds
  7. Complete 3 rounds
⏱ 2-3 minutes 🔥 Energizing ⚠️ Avoid if pregnant
3️⃣

Bhramari (Bee Breath)

Calming breath that reduces anxiety and promotes deep relaxation

How to Practice:

  1. Sit comfortably with eyes closed
  2. Place index fingers on ears to close them
  3. Take a deep breath in through nose
  4. Exhale slowly while making humming sound like a bee
  5. Feel the vibration in your head
  6. Repeat 5-10 times
⏱ 2-3 minutes 😌 Calming 🧘 Meditation Aid
4️⃣

Ujjayi (Ocean Breath)

Victorious breath that builds internal heat and enhances focus

How to Practice:

  1. Sit or stand with spine straight
  2. Slightly constrict the back of your throat
  3. Inhale through nose making soft ocean sound (5 counts)
  4. Exhale through nose with same sound (5 counts)
  5. Keep breath smooth and controlled
  6. Practice for 5-10 minutes
⏱ 5-10 minutes 🔥 Warming 🎯 Focus Builder

💡 Important Guidelines

✓

Practice on empty stomach, preferably in the morning

✓

Sit in a comfortable position with straight spine

✓

Never force the breath - keep it natural and smooth

✓

Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable

⚠️

Consult doctor if you have respiratory conditions

⚠️

Pregnant women should avoid forceful breathing
