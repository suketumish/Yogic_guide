☀️ About Surya Namaskar

Surya Namaskar, or Sun Salutation, is a sequence of 12 powerful yoga poses that pay homage to the sun. This ancient practice combines physical exercise, breathing techniques, and meditation to energize the entire body.

Traditionally performed at sunrise, Surya Namaskar awakens the body, improves flexibility, strengthens muscles, and promotes mental clarity. One complete round consists of 12 poses performed twice (once leading with the right leg, once with the left).

🌞 The 12 Sacred Poses

1

Pranamasana (Prayer Pose)

View Details →

Stand at edge of mat, feet together. Bring palms together at chest in prayer position.

Mantra: Om Mitraya Namaha (Salutations to the friend of all)

🧘 Centering 💚 Heart Chakra
2

Hasta Uttanasana (Raised Arms Pose)

View Details →

Inhale, raise arms overhead, arch back slightly, look up at hands.

Mantra: Om Ravaye Namaha (Salutations to the shining one)

🌬️ Inhale 💪 Stretches abdomen
3

Hasta Padasana (Hand to Foot Pose)

View Details →

Exhale, bend forward from waist, touch floor beside feet. Keep legs straight.

Mantra: Om Suryaya Namaha (Salutations to he who induces activity)

🌬️ Exhale 🦵 Hamstrings
4

Ashwa Sanchalanasana (Equestrian Pose)

View Details →

Inhale, step right leg back, drop knee. Left knee bent at 90°. Look up.

Mantra: Om Bhanave Namaha (Salutations to he who illuminates)

🌬️ Inhale 🦵 Hip flexors
5

Dandasana (Stick Pose)

View Details →

Hold breath, step left leg back. Body in straight line, plank position.

Mantra: Om Khagaya Namaha (Salutations to he who moves in the sky)

⏸️ Hold 💪 Core strength
6

Ashtanga Namaskara (Eight Limbs Pose)

View Details →

Exhale, lower knees, chest, chin to floor. Hips up. Eight points touch ground.

Mantra: Om Pushne Namaha (Salutations to the giver of strength)

🌬️ Exhale 💪 Upper body
7

Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)

View Details →

Inhale, slide forward, raise chest. Arms straight, shoulders back. Look up.

Mantra: Om Hiranya Garbhaya Namaha (Salutations to the golden cosmic self)

🌬️ Inhale 🔙 Spine flexibility
8

Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Dog)

View Details →

Exhale, lift hips up. Form inverted V. Heels toward floor, head between arms.

Mantra: Om Marichaye Namaha (Salutations to the lord of dawn)

🌬️ Exhale 🦵 Calves & hamstrings
9

Ashwa Sanchalanasana (Equestrian Pose)

View Details →

Inhale, step right foot forward between hands. Left knee down. Look up.

Mantra: Om Adityaya Namaha (Salutations to the son of Aditi)

🌬️ Inhale 🦵 Hip flexors
10

Hasta Padasana (Hand to Foot Pose)

Exhale, bring left foot forward. Straighten legs, fold forward.

Mantra: Om Savitre Namaha (Salutations to the stimulating power of the sun)

🌬️ Exhale 🦵 Hamstrings
11

Hasta Uttanasana (Raised Arms Pose)

Inhale, rise up, arms overhead. Arch back slightly, look up.

Mantra: Om Arkaya Namaha (Salutations to he who is fit to be praised)

🌬️ Inhale 💪 Stretches abdomen
12

Pranamasana (Prayer Pose)

Exhale, return to standing. Bring palms together at chest. Complete one round.

Mantra: Om Bhaskaraya Namaha (Salutations to he who leads to enlightenment)

🌬️ Exhale 🙏 Gratitude

💡 Practice Guidelines

✓

Practice on empty stomach, ideally at sunrise

✓

Synchronize breath with each movement

✓

Start with 2-4 rounds, build up gradually

✓

Maintain smooth, flowing transitions

⚠️

Avoid if you have back injuries or high blood pressure

⚠️

Pregnant women should consult doctor first

💡 Note: This completes one half-round. For a complete round, repeat the sequence leading with the left leg in poses 4 and 9. Beginners should start with 2-4 rounds and gradually increase to 12 rounds for maximum benefits.
