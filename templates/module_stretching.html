🧘 Stretching Sequence (12 Poses)

1

Neck Rolls

Gently roll your neck in circular motions to release tension

⏱ 30 seconds 🎯 Neck & Shoulders
2

Shoulder Shrugs

Lift shoulders up to ears, hold, then release down

⏱ 10 reps 🎯 Shoulders
3

Arm Circles

Extend arms and make large circles forward and backward

⏱ 20 circles 🎯 Arms & Shoulders
4

Side Bends

Stand tall, reach one arm overhead and bend to the side

⏱ 30 sec/side 🎯 Obliques & Spine
5

Torso Twists

Rotate upper body left and right, keeping hips stable

⏱ 20 twists 🎯 Spine & Core
6

Cat-Cow Stretch

On hands and knees, alternate arching and rounding spine

⏱ 1 minute 🎯 Spine & Back
7

Child's Pose

Sit back on heels, extend arms forward, rest forehead down

⏱ 1 minute 🎯 Back & Hips
8

Downward Dog

Form inverted V-shape, press heels toward floor

⏱ 45 seconds 🎯 Full Body
9

Forward Fold

Stand and bend forward, reaching toward toes

⏱ 45 seconds 🎯 Hamstrings & Back
10

Hip Circles

Stand with hands on hips, make large circles

⏱ 20 circles 🎯 Hips & Lower Back
11

Quad Stretch

Stand on one leg, pull other foot to glutes

⏱ 30 sec/leg 🎯 Quadriceps
12

Seated Spinal Twist

Sit cross-legged, twist torso to each side

⏱ 30 sec/side 🎯 Spine & Core

💡 Important Tips

✓

Breathe deeply and slowly throughout each stretch

✓

Never force a stretch - go to your comfortable edge

✓

Hold each pose for the recommended duration

✓

Practice on an empty stomach for best results
