## 1. Project Overview

### Vision
Yogic Guide is a comprehensive wellness platform that combines ancient yoga practices with modern AI technology to provide personalized, real-time guidance for practitioners of all levels.

### Key Objectives
- ✅ Provide accurate, real-time pose detection and correction
- ✅ Offer audio guidance throughout practice sessions
- ✅ Track user progress with detailed analytics
- ✅ Support multiple yoga modules (Surya Namaskar, Breathing, Stretching, etc.)
- ✅ Enable admin oversight with comprehensive dashboards
- ✅ Ensure accessibility and responsive design

### Target Users
- **Beginners:** New to yoga, need guidance and corrections
- **Intermediate:** Regular practitioners seeking to improve form
- **Advanced:** Experienced yogis tracking progress and consistency
- **Instructors/Admins:** Managing users and monitoring platform health
